Try it yourself

  1. Wait for the menu: A:START B:STATUS
  2. Click A start, A Bay A, B Fast, 8 100%, C go
  3. Watch the Bay A LEDs fill up (Fast: 20 s for 100%)
  4. Click GRID PEAK: Fast slows to 7 kW, Bay B waits
  5. Click D: emergency stop, LEDs off, buzzer alarm
  6. Click #: reset, alarm off, back to the menu

Your keyboard also works: 0-9, a-d, #, * and g for the grid button.
